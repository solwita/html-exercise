Welcome to GenAI Hub

About GenAI Hub

GenAI Hub is an educational website designed to help students and researchers understand, compare, and use Generative AI tools responsibly. The website provides information about different AI tools, their uses, trust levels, costs, and possible risks such as hallucinations.

What is Generative AI?

Generative AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that can create new content, such as text, images, code, summaries, and research ideas. These tools can support academic work, creativity, software development, and business productivity.

Who is this website for?

This website is mainly aimed at students and researchers who want to choose suitable AI tools for academic and research-related tasks. It may also be useful for creative users, developers, and business users who want a simple comparison of popular tools.

Main Features

  • AI tool comparison table
  • Category filtering for academic, creative, developer, and business tools
  • Trust level and hallucination risk ratings
  • Safe and responsible AI use guidance
  • Information to support better decision-making

Why Responsible AI Use Matters

Although Generative AI can be useful, it can also produce incorrect information, biased content, or unsupported claims. Students and researchers should always check AI-generated information, avoid plagiarism, and protect personal or sensitive data.
