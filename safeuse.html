Safe and Responsible Use of AI

Generative AI can be useful for students and researchers, but it should be used carefully and responsibly.

Why Safe AI Use Matters

AI tools can help with brainstorming, summarising, coding, research support and writing ideas. However, they can also produce incorrect information, biased content or misleading answers. This is why users should always check AI-generated work before relying on it.

Common Risks of Generative AI

  • Hallucinations: AI may create false or inaccurate information.
  • Plagiarism: Students may accidentally submit AI-generated work as their own.
  • Privacy risks: Personal or sensitive data should not be entered into AI tools.
  • Bias: AI outputs can reflect bias from training data.
  • Over-reliance: AI should support learning, not replace independent thinking.

Safe AI Use Checklist

  1. Use AI as a support tool, not as a replacement for your own work.
  2. Check facts using reliable academic or official sources.
  3. Do not copy and paste AI responses directly into assignments.
  4. Avoid entering private, personal or confidential information.
  5. Follow your university’s rules on AI and academic integrity.

How Students Can Use AI Responsibly

Students can use Generative AI for tasks such as planning, understanding difficult topics, creating revision notes and improving writing structure. However, final answers, arguments and conclusions should come from the student’s own understanding.

How Researchers Can Use AI Responsibly

Researchers may use AI to summarise papers, organise notes or explore ideas. However, AI-generated summaries should always be checked against the original sources to avoid relying on inaccurate information.
